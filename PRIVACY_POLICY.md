This Privacy Policy explains how the Application handles information. Use of the Application constitutes consent.

1. Overview
The Application is designed specifically for the Indian market and prioritizes user privacy. We follow a "Privacy-First" architecture where core functionality operates completely offline, ensuring your financial information never leaves your device without your explicit consent.

2. Zero Data Collection (Core Functionality)
No Personal Identifiers: We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as Name, Address, Email, Phone Number, or Date of Birth.
No Financial/Portfolio Data: We do NOT collect, store, or transmit PAN, Bank Account Numbers, UPI IDs, credit history, transaction records, or calculation inputs. All calculator entries and history records remain stored strictly locally on your device.
No Device Fingerprinting: We do NOT track your device or use persistent hardware identifiers for core app functionality.
3. Internet Connectivity & Rationale
While the app performs calculations offline, internet access may be used for:

Market Data: Fetching public NAVs and stock prices from trusted public APIs. This is a local request that does not transmit any user inputs or identifiers.
Security & Integrity: Automated, non-identifiable Play Integrity checks.
Ads: Serving advertisements via Google AdMob.
